# Air Squat
Pattern: squat | Tempo: 3-0-1-0
Start: Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly outward, chest up, core braced.
Movement: Hips back/down; knees track middle toes; controlled descent.
End: Controlled depth with heels grounded and neutral spine; drive to standing.
Avoid: knee collapse, heel lift, excessive lumbar rounding.
